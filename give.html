Generosity

Your Giving Changes Lives

Every gift funds the work of the gospel — supporting families, sending the Word, and shining light into our community of Obanikoro and beyond.

Why We Give

Worship Through Generosity

At Shepherdhill, giving is not a transaction — it's worship. We give because God has first given to us, supremely in His Son. Every tithe and offering advances the gospel and ministers to people whose lives are being shaped here.

"Each one must give as he has decided in his heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver."
— 2 Corinthians 9:7
