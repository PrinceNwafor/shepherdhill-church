Our Ministries

There's a place for you to grow, serve, and belong. Explore the heart of Shepherdhill's life together — for every age and every season.

For Every Age

Find Your Family at Shepherdhill

👶

Children's Ministry

Age-appropriate Sunday school and worship for babies through age 12 — a safe, fun, Christ-centered space.

🎓

Teens & Youth

Equipping the next generation to live boldly for Christ in school, online, and in every relationship.

💼

Young Adults

For students and young professionals navigating purpose, career, relationships, and faith.

💍

Marriage & Family

Strengthening homes through teaching, retreats, and pre-marital counselling.

👩

Women of Grace

Where women are equipped to live in their God-given identity, calling, and gifting.

👨

Men of Glory

Brotherhood, accountability, and discipleship for men of every season.

Worship & Service

Where You Can Serve

🎵

Choir & Worship Team

Vocalists, instrumentalists, and worship leaders carrying the people into God's presence.

🎥

Media & Technical

Sound, lights, camera, and online streaming — extending the gospel through every screen.

🙌

Ushers & Hospitality

First impressions matter. Our ushers make sure every guest feels welcomed and at home.

📖

Discipleship

Small groups, Bible study cells, and one-on-one mentoring for every new and growing believer.

🤲

Prayer Ministry

A standing intercessory team praying daily for our church, city, and nations.

Outreach & Mercy
