Your First Step

Plan Your Visit

We'd love to welcome you to Shepherdhill. Here's everything you need to feel comfortable, confident, and at home on your first Sunday.

What to Expect

Your First Sunday

1

Arrive 15 Minutes Early

Give yourself time to park, find your seat, and meet a friendly greeter at the entrance.

2

Look for the Welcome Desk

Pick up a welcome pack with a guide to the service, our ministries, and a small gift.

3

Worship with Us

Singing, prayer, the Word, and communion (on the first Sunday). Just relax — there's no pressure to do anything you're not ready for.

4

Stay and Say Hi

After service, our pastoral team would love to meet you over light refreshments.

Tell Us You're Coming

We'll have someone ready to welcome you personally.

FAQs

Common Questions

What should I wear?

Whatever you're comfortable in. Some dress up, some dress down — God looks at the heart, and so do we.

Where do my kids go?

Children have age-appropriate ministry running parallel to the main service. Our team will help you check them in.

How long is the service?

About 90 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the Sunday. Worship, the Word, and time to respond.

Will I be singled out?

Not at all. We'll warmly welcome you, but you can stay anonymous as long as you'd like.
