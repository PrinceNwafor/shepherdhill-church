Our Story

A Sanctuary of
Grace and Glory

For over four decades, Shepherdhill Baptist Church has stood in Obanikoro, Lagos as a beacon of God's grace — welcoming the searching, restoring the broken, and raising a people who carry His glory into every sphere of life.

Our Mission

To lead every soul into the fullness of life found in Jesus Christ — through biblical teaching, authentic worship, deep community, and Spirit-led service.

We exist because we believe the gospel of Jesus Christ is the most beautiful message ever entrusted to humanity. And we believe a local church — when it is truly alive — is the hope of the world.

What You'll Find Here

  • Bible-soaked preaching — every message rooted in Scripture, applied to real life.
  • Genuine community — a family that prays for you, walks with you, and shows up.
  • Spirit-led worship — moments where Heaven feels close.
  • Service beyond the walls — touching Lagos and reaching nations.
Our Senior Pastor

Rev. Dr. Israel O. Kristilere

Rev. Dr. Israel O. Kristilere is an ordained and anointed minister of God's Word and Power. He currently serves as the Senior Pastor of Shepherdhill Baptist Church — the Sanctuary of Grace and Glory — Obanikoro, Lagos.

Prior to this assignment, he was the pioneer full-time pastor of the Amazing Grace Baptist Church, Ilupeju, and the Senior Pastor of Ijero Baptist Church, Ebute-Metta (West), Lagos.

He is the Founding President of Friends of Israel, Nigeria (FOIN), President of the Christian Tourism Practitioners Association of Nigeria (CTPAN) — the umbrella body for all Private Christian Pilgrimage Operators in Nigeria — and founder of Oasis of Faith Ministries (1998), reaching the world with the full gospel of the Lord Jesus Christ.

His passion is to see lives transformed by the Word, to pastor a people of grace, and to release the glory of God across the nations.
