Testimonies of Grace

Lives Touched. Stories Told.

Behind every Sunday is a story. Here, members of the Shepherdhill family share what God has done — in healing, restoration, breakthrough, and quiet, ordinary grace.

From Our Members

What God Has Done

Restoration

"I came to Shepherdhill broken — my marriage was in pieces and I was barely holding on. Through the prayer ministry and Rev. Dr. Kristilere’s counselling, my husband and I rebuilt our home. Today we serve together in this same church."

AO
Adeola O.
Member since 2019 · Marriage restored
Healing

"After two years of medical reports we did not want to hear, the church stood with us in prayer through every Thursday Thanksgiving service. God’s mercy came through. Today I am alive, whole, and worshipping with my children."

MO
Mrs. Mary O.
Member since 2015 · Healing testimony
Breakthrough

"I walked into Shepherdhill in my NYSC year, not knowing what was next. The Word preached here straightened my path. Three years later, I have a job, a calling, and a family I never thought possible."

TA
Tunde A.
Member since 2022 · Career breakthrough
Family

"We moved from another city and were looking for a place to raise our kids in the faith. Shepherdhill welcomed us like we had always been here. The children’s ministry is excellent — my kids ask to come every Sunday."

CN
Chioma & Emeka N.
Family · Members since 2021
Salvation

"I came to a service just to please a friend. I left a different person. The presence of God in that room broke me open. I gave my life to Christ that day, and have never been the same since."

SO
Samuel O.
Member since 2023 · New birth in Christ
Provision

"Year of Triple Exaltation — and I can testify. Doors that had been shut for years opened in one season. God lifted me from the dust as Pastor preached, and today the testimony speaks for itself."

FE
Funke E.
Member since 2017 · Open doors
