Our Heart

A Sanctuary of
Grace and Glory

Whether you are stepping in for the first time, or have walked with us for years — there is room for you at Shepherdhill.

For decades, our doors in Obanikoro have opened to welcome the weary, the seeking, and the searching. We believe in a God who meets people in the middle of every season, and our mission is simple: to lead lives into the fullness of His grace and glory.

Meet Our Senior Pastor

Rev. Dr. Israel O. Kristilere

"The silence of God is not the absence of God. Divine delay is not divine denial. Your Father will show up."

Rev. Dr. Israel O. Kristilere is the Senior Pastor of Shepherdhill Baptist Church, the Sanctuary of Grace and Glory. An ordained minister of God's Word and Power, he previously served as the pioneer pastor of Amazing Grace Baptist Church, Ilupeju, and Senior Pastor of Ijero Baptist Church, Ebute-Metta (West).

He is the Founding President of Friends of Israel, Nigeria (FOIN), President of the Christian Tourism Practitioners Association of Nigeria (CTPAN), and founder of Oasis of Faith Ministries — reaching the world with the full gospel of Jesus Christ.
