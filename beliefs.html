What We Believe

Our Statement of Faith

Rooted in Scripture, anchored in the historic Baptist tradition, and alive in the power of the Holy Spirit — here is what holds us together.

01

The Holy Scriptures

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God — the supreme authority for faith, life, and practice. It is the lens through which we see every truth.

02

One God in Three Persons

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three Persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — equal in power and glory, distinct in role and relationship.

03

The Person and Work of Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man. He lived a sinless life, died on the cross for our sins, rose bodily from the dead, and is coming again. Salvation is in His name alone.

04

The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit convicts of sin, regenerates the believer, indwells every Christian, and empowers us for godly living and Spirit-led service. We embrace His gifts and fruit.

05

Salvation by Grace Through Faith

Salvation is the free gift of God, received by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone — not by works, merit, or human effort, lest anyone should boast.

06

Baptism and the Lord's Supper

We practice believer's baptism by full immersion as a public declaration of faith. The Lord's Supper is observed in remembrance of Christ's atoning death.

07

The Church

The Church is the body of Christ — a family of believers called to worship, fellowship, discipleship, ministry, and the Great Commission of making disciples of all nations.

08

The Return of Christ

We believe in the personal, visible return of our Lord Jesus Christ — and we live in joyful, holy expectation of that day when every knee shall bow.
